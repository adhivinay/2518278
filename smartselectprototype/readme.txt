## 📱 Android App

- **What’s included:**  
  - The Android Studio project (source code)
  - Optionally, a prebuilt `SmartSelect.apk` for easy install

- **Run on Phone:**  
  1. Copy the `.apk` to your device and tap to install.
  2. Allow "Unknown Sources" if prompted.
  3. Open the app and use it with live or mocked data.

- **Run on PC (Emulator):**  
  1. Use **Android Studio Emulator** or **Bluestacks/NoxPlayer**.
  2. Drag & drop the `.apk` onto the emulator to install.
